## Key decisions

- `claimSubmission` enforces `READY -> SUBMITTING` CAS and records the attempt before committing the transaction and calling the port.
- Settlement port errors map to `POSSIBLY_SUBMITTED` -> `UNKNOWN` with reconciliation enqueued; blind retries are prohibited.
- Job keys serve as scheduling hygiene; deduplication and submission ownership are strictly guaranteed by PostgreSQL row locks and CAS versioning.
